Privacy Policy for Thread

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Thread is a simple personal note-capture tool. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

What Thread collects

Nothing. Thread does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind. There are no servers involved — the app doesn't send any information anywhere.

Where your notes are stored

Every note ("thread") you save stays entirely on your own device, in your browser's or app's local storage. It is never uploaded, backed up to the cloud, or made accessible to anyone else, including the developer of this app.

Microphone / dictation

Thread does not access your microphone directly. Voice input happens through your device's own keyboard dictation feature, which is controlled entirely by your phone's operating system (Google or Apple), not by this app.

Third parties

Thread does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing individual threads in the app, or by clearing the app's storage/data through your device settings. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app developer via the contact details listed on the Play Store listing.
